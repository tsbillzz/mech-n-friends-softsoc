## How to Use the App

Here’s a quick guide for judges to get the most out of the demo:

1.  **Explore the Map**: The app opens with an interactive map centered on the campus. You can pan, zoom, and click on any building marker.
2.  **View a Building's PCs**: Click a building marker (e.g., "Fisher Library") to navigate to the **Building View**, where you'll see a grid of all PCs and their live status.
3.  **Find the Perfect PC**:
    *   From the main map screen, click the **sliders icon** (top right) to open the **"Find a PC"** dialog.
    *   **Select Software**: Choose one or more software applications you need.
    *   **Find a Group Spot**: Toggle the "Enable Group Study Finder" switch to find adjacent available computers.
    *   Click the **"Find PC"** button. The app will use your browser's location to find the nearest building that matches your criteria and highlight it with a bouncing animation on the map.
4.  **Predict Future Availability** (there is no data so this does not work at the moment):
    *   Navigate into any **Building View**.
    *   On the right-hand side, you'll find the **AI Availability Forecaster**.
    *   Select a day and time, then click **"Predict Availability"** to get an AI-generated forecast.
